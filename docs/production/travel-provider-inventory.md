## Capability boundary

Mapbox Geocoding v6 is not a POI search product, so arbitrary restaurants, lodging, and trailheads may remain ambiguous or unavailable rather than being silently selected. Mapbox transit is unsupported. OpenWeather dates beyond its response horizon are unavailable, and polar sunrise/sunset omission is explicit. NPS alert records without effective dates are treated as currently returned official notices with retrieval time, not as proof of a future state.

RIDB support currently covers recreation areas and official links. The normalized contract can represent facilities, campgrounds, tours, permits, and availability, but Phase 6A does not claim official live inventory for those entities unless a reviewed endpoint returns it. Public Recreation.gov links are safe fallbacks; scraping is forbidden.

No Google, hotel, restaurant, booking-site, or unofficial provider scraping is used.
